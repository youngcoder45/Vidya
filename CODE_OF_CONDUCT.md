# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

We are committed to providing a welcoming, respectful, and constructive environment for everyone who participates in the SchoolOS project.

Contributors, maintainers, reviewers, issue reporters, and community members are expected to interact professionally and in good faith.

---

## Expected Behavior

We encourage participants to:

* Be respectful and professional.
* Communicate clearly and constructively.
* Assume good faith when interpreting others' contributions.
* Focus criticism on ideas, implementations, and technical decisions rather than individuals.
* Welcome different perspectives and levels of experience.
* Provide actionable and constructive feedback.
* Respect project maintainers, contributors, and community members.
* Keep discussions relevant to the project.
* Take responsibility for mistakes and correct them when necessary.
* Respect the privacy and personal information of others.

Technical disagreement is expected in software development. Differences should be resolved through evidence, reasoning, experimentation, and respectful discussion.

---

## Unacceptable Behavior

The following behavior is not acceptable:

* Harassment, discrimination, or intimidation.
* Personal attacks or insults.
* Threats of violence or harm.
* Hate speech or derogatory comments targeting individuals or groups.
* Deliberate disruption of discussions or project activities.
* Trolling, baiting, or repeated bad-faith arguments.
* Publishing someone's private or personally identifying information without permission.
* Sexual harassment or unwanted sexual attention.
* Spam or excessive self-promotion unrelated to the project.
* Deliberately misleading contributors or maintainers.
* Repeatedly ignoring project contribution guidelines.
* Any other behavior that would reasonably make the project environment hostile or unsafe.

---

## Technical Discussions

SchoolOS is an engineering project, and technical disagreements are expected.

When discussing an implementation:

1. Clearly identify the problem.
2. Explain the reasoning behind your position.
3. Provide evidence, benchmarks, documentation, or examples where possible.
4. Consider alternative approaches.
5. Keep criticism focused on the technical issue.
6. Accept the final decision of maintainers when a decision is required.

Disagreement does not justify personal attacks.

---

## Maintainer Responsibilities

Project maintainers are responsible for:

* Maintaining a professional project environment.
* Reviewing reported conduct concerns.
* Applying these guidelines consistently.
* Taking appropriate action when unacceptable behavior occurs.
* Protecting the privacy of people involved in conduct reports where reasonably possible.

Maintainers may remove, edit, or reject comments, issues, pull requests, commits, or other contributions that violate this Code of Conduct.

---

## Enforcement

Depending on the severity and circumstances of a violation, maintainers may take actions including:

1. A private or public warning.
2. Removal of inappropriate content.
3. Temporary restriction from participating in project discussions.
4. Temporary suspension of contribution privileges.
5. Permanent removal from project participation.
6. Reporting serious or unlawful conduct to the appropriate authorities where necessary.

Enforcement decisions will be based on the circumstances of each incident.

---

## Reporting

If you experience or witness behavior that violates this Code of Conduct, please report it privately to the project maintainer.

**Do not create a public GitHub issue for sensitive conduct reports.**

When reporting an incident, provide:

* A description of what happened.
* The relevant GitHub issue, pull request, discussion, or other context.
* The approximate date and time.
* Any relevant screenshots or evidence.
* The usernames of the people involved, if known.

Please avoid sharing unnecessary personal information.

For security vulnerabilities, follow the security reporting process described in [`CONTRIBUTING.md`](CONTRIBUTING.md) instead.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to project-related interactions, including:

* GitHub issues
* Pull requests
* Discussions
* Code reviews
* Commit comments
* Documentation contributions
* Project meetings or events
* Official SchoolOS community spaces
* Other spaces where an individual is representing the SchoolOS project

## Attribution

This Code of Conduct is inspired by common standards used by open-source software communities and has been adapted for the SchoolOS project.

---

## Final Note

The goal of this Code of Conduct is not to prevent disagreement. **Good technical disagreement is essential to building better software.**

The goal is to ensure that disagreements remain focused on the work, are supported by reason and evidence, and are conducted with respect.

**Be technical. Be constructive. Be respectful.**
